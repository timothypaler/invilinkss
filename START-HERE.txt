INVILINKS — READY TO UPLOAD

index.html is directly at the top level of this ZIP.
Extract the ZIP and upload all the extracted files and folders together.
Keep assets/, vendor/, pricing/, how-it-works/ and photo-credits/ alongside index.html.
Do not upload only index.html; the accompanying files are required.
There is no build step.

LOCAL PREVIEW
From the extracted folder, run: python3 -m http.server 8000
Open http://localhost:8000

EDITING
Shared page content: scripts/build-pages.py
Styles: experience.css
Menus and inquiries: app.js
Scrolling: experience.js
Page-loading animation: transitions.js
After edits run: python3 scripts/build-pages.py
It regenerates the HTML directly in this folder.

Contains the latest social links, scroll fix, matching package designs,
and flexible opening-style descriptions. Keep the included licenses and credits.
