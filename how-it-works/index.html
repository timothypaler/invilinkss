GOOD TO KNOW

A few
details.

Are the samples templates?+

No. They show design, animation and feature possibilities. The whole website can be designed in a different direction for your event. An envelope, ribbon or other opening direction is not restricted to a particular package.

Can any package be used for any occasion?+

Yes. Classic, Signature and Immersive describe the scope of graphics, interactions and animation. Themes and opening styles can be adapted across all three packages. All are available for weddings, debuts, birthdays, kids’ parties, company events and other occasions.

How do I order?+

Message the official InviLinks Facebook or Instagram page with your event, date and ideas. Open How to order to choose Facebook or Instagram and prepare your inquiry.

What about RSVP, music and other interactions?+

RSVP and background music are included in every package. Choose RSVP through text message or Google Forms, and tell us the song you want. The sample forms are demonstrations and do not send responses to a host.

How many revisions, and when will it be ready?+

Delivery is 1–2 days. Classic includes 2 revision rounds; Signature and Immersive include 3. Additional revisions may incur a fee. Each round is one consolidated set of feedback.

Does the invitation expire?+

No expiration. Your invitation has no scheduled expiry date.
